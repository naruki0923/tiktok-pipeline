- App review の説明 (986/1000):

Video Stats Sync is a private, single-user tool used by the owner of one TikTok account to analyse the performance of their own videos. It is not distributed to other users.

Login Kit (user.info.basic): The owner clicks "Log in with TikTok" on the tool's web page, authorizes the app and is redirected back. The tool stores the token locally and shows the owner's display name and avatar to confirm which account is connected.

Display API (video.list): After login, the tool calls /v2/video/list/ to fetch the owner's own videos with title, share_url, create_time, view_count, like_count, comment_count and share_count. The numbers are saved to a local file and used to see which topics performed well. Nothing is posted, edited or deleted on TikTok, and nothing is shared with third parties.

The demo video shows the full flow in the sandbox: opening the tool page, logging in with TikTok, granting the two scopes, returning to the tool, and the fetched video list with view counts.
