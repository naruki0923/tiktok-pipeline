Video Stats Sync

Video Stats Sync is a private, single-user tool. It lets the owner of a TikTok account sign in with TikTok and fetch the list of their own videos together with public statistics (view, like, comment and share counts). The numbers are stored locally on the owner's computer and used to see which topics perform well.

The tool is not offered to the public. It does not post, edit or delete anything on TikTok.
